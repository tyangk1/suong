# SƯƠNG: trà của sương sớm (ink-wash tea landing page)

Run: `node .claude/skills/scroll-craft/scripts/serve.mjs --root builds/suong --port 4514` (from `landing-lab/`), then open http://localhost:4514

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-suong-tea-design.md` · Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-suong-tea.md`

## What makes it different from SOLE/LAB and THOCK
- **The mood is slow and quiet:** ink on dó paper, lots of white space, one vermilion accent. There is no sound, no physics and no keyboard control. All copy is in Vietnamese.
- **Loader:** a drop of ink falls, blooms on the paper and settles into the seal. It runs once per session.
- **Hero:** three ink layers (far ridges, tea hills, tea branch) drift apart on scroll with a faint mouse sway, while two mist bands drift across.
- **Bốn chương (Hái → Héo → Sao → Pha):** a pinned section in which each painting soaks into the paper as you scroll.
  - Inline SVG masks: blobs pass through feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap + blur + alpha contrast.
  - The mask is dropped once the reveal completes.
  - On phones the chapters are stacked, and each reveals when it enters the screen.
- **Chọn một chén:**
  - Picking a tea pours a stream into a hand-drawn cup, which fills with that tea's liquor colour.
  - The package photo bleeds in with the same ink reveal.
  - The Trà Sen pick brings in a lotus.
- **Pha một ấm:** a brush line draws itself between the 4 steps, and the numbers follow the chosen tea.
- **Đặt trước:** the price updates live (in VND), and the choice is remembered on the device.
  - The "Đặt trước — sắp mở" button only shows a note: nothing is charged and nothing is sent.
- **Steam:** faint steam rises from the cursor on desktop.

## Pipeline
- **Gemini images:** 26 images (`IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`), generated by hand in the Gemini app.
  - Ink paintings are on pure white and are put on the page with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.
  - `hill-far` and `hill-mid` came out swapped; `tools/clean.ps1` swaps them back.
- **PhotoCraft** (`tools/clean.ps1`): `paint.remove` on the Gemini ✦ for 11 photos.
  - `set-still` is cropped at the bottom instead, because the fill invented a tray leg.
- **PIL** (`tools/prep.py`):
  - white point 240 → 255 on the ink paintings;
  - tileable dó-paper fibre texture;
  - periodic mist band;
  - ragged edge mask for the photos and paintings;
  - drops and strokes split out with alpha;
  - WebP at desktop and `-m` sizes.
- **DesignCraft** (`tools/logo.py`, headless): the vermilion seal "SƯƠNG / TRÀ SỚM" in Noto Serif Bold, aged in PIL into a stamped texture.
  - Also the one-letter "S" seal for the favicon, and the OG 1200×630.
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond and Be Vietnam Pro, both OFL, self-hosted woff2 with the latin and vietnamese subsets. Licences are in `assets/fonts/`.
- **Libraries:** GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger (GreenSock standard licence) and Lenis 1.1.0 (MIT), vendored in `vendor/`.

## Gotchas
- `mix-blend-mode: multiply` only sees the backdrop inside its stacking context. Pinned layers, faded layers and **masked** elements are isolated groups, so those containers carry `background: var(--paper)`.
- Cormorant uses old-style digits by default. `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums` must come *after* any `font:` shorthand, because the shorthand resets it.

## Checks
Run `tools/shots.js` via the Playwright MCP `browser_run_code_unsafe` while the site is served. On 2026-11-10 it gave these results:
- **Desktop, mobile and both reduced-motion modes:** 0 console errors, 0 failed requests, no overflow.
- **First load** (decoded): about 1.3 MB on mobile and 1.4 MB on desktop.
- **Tea picker:** the 4 liquor colours are correct, and a rapid switch ends on the last choice.
- **Pre-order:** Shan Tuyết 100 g × 2 gives 960.000đ, and the note shows.
- **Storage blocked:** works without errors.
